mark are not part of that grant.

- **Unchanged builds** of a release, and builds of this repository's source as it stands, may be called Chatkord.
- **A modified version** (a fork, a build with patches of its own) must not be called Chatkord or use the mark. Give
  it a name of its own; "based on Chatkord" in its description is fine.
- **Writing about Chatkord**, linking to it, or saying that a server runs it, needs no permission.
- **A server you host** may say it works with Chatkord when members use an unchanged release or this repository's source.

Anything else, ask before you use the name or the mark.
